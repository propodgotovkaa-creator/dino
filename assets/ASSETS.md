# Графика

Все девять исходных изображений созданы встроенным инструментом image_gen в этой сессии. Для игры изображения перекодированы в WebP; прозрачность сохранена. Сторонние изображения и удалённые URL в игре не используются.

Промпты (содержательные спецификации):

- **rex.webp** — Scientific-educational museum-quality full Tyrannosaurus rex fossil skeleton, lateral view facing left, massive fenestrated skull and teeth, tiny two-fingered arms, powerful hindlimbs, long counterbalance tail, fossil ivory/ochre bone, transparent background, no rods, pedestal, labels, cartoon.
- **triceratops.webp** — Scientific-educational full Triceratops fossil skeleton, lateral view facing left, two brow horns and nasal horn, massive frill and skull, deep ribcage, four sturdy legs, tapering tail, real fossil texture, transparent background, no supports or labels.
- **stegosaurus.webp** — Full Stegosaurus fossil skeleton, lateral view facing left, very small low head, arched ribcage, alternating dorsal plates, shorter forelegs and longer hindlegs, long tail with four thagomizer spikes, weathered ivory/ochre bone, transparent background.
- **velociraptor.webp** — Full Velociraptor mongoliensis fossil skeleton, lateral view facing left, low elongated skull, S-curved neck, slender ribs, long three-fingered arms, raised sickle toe claws, long stiff tail, museum photographic reconstruction, transparent background.
- **expedition.webp** — Photorealistic wide dry sandstone valley excavation, layered rocky mountains, golden morning light, cracked sandy earth, excavation pit right, wooden brush and field notebook on equipment bag bottom right, calm space left, no people, writing or UI.
- **soil.webp** — Photorealistic overhead compact dry sandy soil, fine golden tan sand, irregular pebbles, branching cracks, mineral specks, uniform soil across full frame, no bones or tools.
- **brush.webp** — Photorealistic single paleontologist flat soft brush seen from above, vertical dark wooden handle at top, silver ferrule, broad tan natural bristles pointing down, transparent background.
- **lab.webp** — Photorealistic paleontology conservation lab, cabinets, microscopes, brushes, warm window light, large empty muted olive-grey worktable in foreground, no people or writing.
- **museum.webp** — Photorealistic limestone natural-history museum hall, warm spotlights, four empty stone specimen plinths in two rows, no skeletons or people; assembled game fossils are overlaid separately.

Общий стиль: реалистичные материалы и освещение, без мультяшных эффектов. Образы не являются научно проверенными сканами. Справочные музейные материалы для проверки характерных признаков: [NHM Dino Directory](https://www.nhm.ac.uk/discover/dino-directory), [NHM Dinosaur collection](https://www.nhm.ac.uk/our-science/services/collections/palaeontology/dinosaurs.html).
